How it works

Six steps from folder to map.

01

Point. Scan. Index.

Paste an absolute path to any local repository. Cartographer parses every Python, JavaScript, and TypeScript file — classes, functions, imports, cyclomatic complexity. GitPython mines up to 500 commits for churn.

02

From flat files to a knowledge graph.

Every file becomes a node. Every import becomes an edge. A full Neo4j graph of your true dependency architecture — not the folder structure.

03

Find what's fragile before it breaks.

A composite risk score: 45% fan-in, 25% complexity, 20% Git churn, 10% fan-out. Jev enhances it with semantic scoring — flagging chokepoints, security-critical files and abandoned code.

04

Deep-dive into any file.

Click a node to open the Inspector: risk components, AI summary, symbols, imports, dependents, external dependencies and a refactor-safety assessment.

05

Ask your codebase anything.

Press Cmd+K. Ask “Where does authentication happen?” Jev routes the query, Neo4j retrieves structural evidence, Gemini reasons over it — you get a grounded answer with clickable file links.

06

Know what breaks before you break it.

Trace up to 6 hops through the dependency graph. The Jev safety gate tells you if a single PR is safe, estimates blast radius (1–10) and recommends a strategy from 8 options.

The two-tier AI brain

Two AI systems. One mission.

A fast structured decision engine and a deep generative reasoner, working in concert.

System One

Fast & structured.

TypeSafe Jev is the decision engine. With three primitives — Choice, Noul and Score — it powers 10 integration bonds that classify, score and route in milliseconds.

Deep & generative.

Gemini is the reasoning layer: architectural summaries, 768-dimensional embeddings for semantic search, RAG answers to free-form questions, and detailed impact narratives.
